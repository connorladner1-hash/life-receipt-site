What it does

The things nobody adds up

A dashboard shows you a chart and you forget it. A receipt shows you a total and you don't.

Line 01

Screen time

Ring up the apps that ate your evening. Each one gets a line, an hour count, and a dollar figure based on what your time is actually worth.

Line 02

Delivery and impulse spending

The $12 you didn't think about, four times a week. Logged as line items, totalled at the bottom, and carried into a weekly figure you can't round down.

Line 03

Sleep debt

Read from Apple Health, with your permission. The hour you lost to one more episode shows up on the same bill as everything else.

Total

One number, once a day

The receipt prints line by line and the total lands with a thud. It's an image you can save and send, because the whole point is that it's hard to look away from.

Tomorrow

A fresh receipt

Yesterday closes out at midnight. There's a new one every morning, and a trend line showing whether the totals are going up or down.

The refunds ledger

You can't undo yesterday.
You can claim a refund.

Every receipt comes with a refund you have to earn. Walk 2,000 steps, log a workout, hit the target you set — LifeReceipt checks it against Apple Health, then credits the time and money back.

Nothing is honour-system. If Health didn't record it, it doesn't count.

What you claim rolls into a lifetime total, and that number only ever goes one direction.
