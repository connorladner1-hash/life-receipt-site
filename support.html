Subscriptions and billing

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so cancelling happens on your device, not here. Open Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → LifeReceipt → Cancel Subscription.

Cancel during the free trial and you keep access until the trial ends, with nothing charged. Cancel a paid subscription and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

I want a refund

Apple issues all refunds for App Store purchases; we can't process them on our side. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If Apple turns you down and you think the charge was a genuine mistake, email us anyway and we'll do what we can.

I paid but the app still shows a paywall

Open Settings inside LifeReceipt and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account that bought the subscription. If it still won't unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

I was charged twice

Check your purchase history in Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → View Account → Purchase History. If there's a genuine duplicate, send us a screenshot and we'll help you get it reversed through Apple.

Your account and your data

Delete my account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the receipts, refunds, and logged entries attached to it.

You can also email support@life-receipt.com from the address on your account with the subject line Delete my account, and we'll do it within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription — Apple controls that. Cancel it separately using the steps above.

Get a copy of my data

Email us from your account address and we'll send an export of what we hold.

Can I use LifeReceipt on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and restore purchases on the second device.

Using the app

My refund won't unlock

Refunds only credit when Apple Health has actually recorded the activity, which can lag by a few minutes after a walk or workout. Pull down to refresh on the Refunds tab. If Health shows the activity and LifeReceipt doesn't, email us with a screenshot of both.

My receipt is wrong

Tap the line item to edit it and the total recalculates. Yesterday's receipt closes at midnight, so edit before then.

I have an idea, or something is broken

Send it to support@life-receipt.com. Bug reports with a screenshot get fixed fastest.
